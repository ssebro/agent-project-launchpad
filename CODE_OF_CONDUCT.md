# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and considerate in issues, reviews, and discussions.
Critique ideas and code constructively. Welcome contributors with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and publishing
someone's private information are not acceptable. Do not share private agent
conversations without the participants' permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation to protect
the community. Raise concerns with a maintainer privately when possible; otherwise
request a private contact without publishing personal details.
